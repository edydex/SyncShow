Second preview of consistent slide typography, built on the 2.1.1 live translation fixes. It replaces 2.1.2-preview.1.

- Scripture verse numbers sit directly beside the first word, without the previous visible space. Numbers remain attached to that word when wrapping; saved emphasis and the original Bible text are preserved.
- Verse numbers 10, 20, 30 and 100–109 now draw their superscript zero in the same style and size as the digit beside it. The bundled font had no superscript zero, so it previously came from another font and looked taller and heavier.
- All lyric slides within a song share the largest fitted font size allowed by the selected preset or manual size. Short verses no longer enlarge independently. Song title slides keep their separate styling.
- While translation captions are shown as a ticker or lower third, every slide of a song now shares one smaller size that fits above the captions, instead of each slide shrinking on its own. Songs that already fit above the captions keep their normal size.
- Singer lyrics also keep one fitted size per song, using their own current-text area and complete source lyrics. The next-slide cue remains visible below them.
- Fixed in this preview: preview.1's built-in planner (Prepare offline and Show → Adjust) had lost the 2.1.1 Screens picker and "Local presentation" status used by Start Blank, could show every library empty when a single library failed to load, and could give two services created on the same day the same ID. The planner is back to its 2.1.1 behaviour, with the new typography.

After updating, reopen the service in Prepare, save, and go to Load to rebuild its slides.

Known trade-off: the song size is the largest that fits every slide, so one very long verse makes the whole song smaller. Split very long verses into two slides.

This is a testing preview for Windows x64, macOS Apple silicon and Intel, and Linux x64. Stable 2.1.1 remains Latest.

## Church projector checklist

1. A song with a short verse and a long verse: on the main screens, all lyric slides are the same size; the singer screen also keeps one size of its own.
2. Scripture with verses 9, 10, 11 and 19, 20, 21: the numbers touch the first word, and the "0" in 10 and 20 matches the digit beside it.
3. Turn on translation captions (ticker, then lower third) during a song: all slides of the song stay one size while captions show, and return to the normal size when captions stop.
4. Slides inside and outside a translated cue region: slides outside keep the full screen.
5. Start Blank: the Screens picker appears, and the new presentation shows "Local presentation" instead of a status.
