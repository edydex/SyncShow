Preview of consistent slide typography, built on the 2.1.1 live translation fixes.

- Scripture verse numbers sit directly beside the first word, without the previous visible space. Numbers remain attached to that word when wrapping; saved emphasis and the original Bible text are preserved.
- All lyric slides within a song share the largest fitted font size allowed by the selected preset or manual size. Short verses no longer enlarge independently. Song title slides keep their separate styling.
- Singer lyrics also keep one fitted size per song, using their own current-text area and complete source lyrics. The next-slide cue remains visible below them.
- Community Prepare, the bundled local editor, and SyncShow output use the same typography rules. Existing prepared packages remain readable.

After updating, reopen the service in Prepare, save, and go to Load to rebuild its slides. Check a short verse followed by the densest bilingual verse, then a Scripture slide on the audience and singer screens. Existing translation defaults and cues continue to use the 2.1.1 behavior.

This is a testing preview for Windows x64, macOS Apple silicon and Intel, and Linux x64. Stable 2.1.1 remains Latest. The libre.show move is reserved for 2.2.
