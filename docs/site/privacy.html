Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the SyncShow desktop application and this public website handle information, with specific detail about optional Google Drive access.

Effective and last updated: July 23, 2026
The short version

SyncShow is local-first. It connects directly from your computer to Google Drive only when you choose that option. It does not send your presentations or Google credentials to a SyncShow-operated server, sell user data, serve ads, or use your content to train AI.

1. Who operates SyncShow

SyncShow is an open-source project provided by Excited Fellas LLC (“SyncShow,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). Questions or privacy requests can be sent to o.maiovskyi@gmail.com.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to the SyncShow desktop application and the static SyncShow project website. It does not replace the privacy policies of Google, GitHub, Microsoft, LibreOffice, your church, or another service you choose to use.

3. Google Drive data SyncShow accesses

Google Drive integration is optional. You can use SyncShow with files selected from your computer and never connect a Google account.

Connection Data accessed Permission and limits
Private Google Drive folder The selected folder’s name and identifier; presentation file names, identifiers, types, dates, sizes, checksums, capabilities, and content; a resource key when Google supplies one; and OAuth access and refresh tokens. SyncShow requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This is narrow, per-file access to content you select, share with, open with, or create through SyncShow. SyncShow does not request access to every file in your Drive.
Public Drive folder link Folder and presentation data that the owner made available to anyone with the link, including the same file metadata and content needed to load the service. No Google sign-in or OAuth token is used. This connection is view-only in SyncShow.
Google account profile None through the current Google integration. SyncShow does not request Google profile, email, contacts, calendar, Gmail, browsing-history, or advertising-data scopes.

Google’s authorization screen identifies the exact permission requested. You decide whether to authorize it and which folder to select.

4. How SyncShow uses Google Drive data

SyncShow uses Drive data only to provide presentation features you request:

  • list supported presentation files inside the folder you selected;
  • match a coherent set of service files by configured role, filename, and service date;
  • download selected PowerPoint or Google Slides content to the local computer;
  • convert and display that content on outputs controlled by the operator;
  • keep a verified local copy so a prepared service can continue if the network is unavailable; and
  • when available in your version, create or update files only after an administrator enables Drive publishing and an operator deliberately chooses a Publish action.

Automatic loading does not create, edit, overwrite, move, or delete Google Drive files. SyncShow does not use Google Drive data for advertising, profiling, credit decisions, surveillance, or training general-purpose artificial intelligence models.

5. Where data is processed and stored

On your computer

Drive file metadata, downloaded presentations, converted slide images, integrity checksums, service manifests, and application settings are processed and stored in SyncShow’s local application-data and cache directories. These local copies make the live presentation workflow faster and resilient to a connection failure.

A private Drive refresh token is encrypted using the operating system credential protection exposed through Electron’s secure storage. SyncShow refuses to save a private token when secure credential storage is unavailable, including an insecure Linux basic_text backend. Short-lived access tokens are held in application memory and discarded when the application exits.

No SyncShow cloud account or content server

The desktop application communicates directly with Google’s authorization and Drive services. Google user data and presentation content are not uploaded to a server operated by SyncShow or Excited Fellas LLC.

This website

This is a static website. It contains no sign-in, forms, analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, or third-party scripts, and it does not read data from the SyncShow desktop application. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process ordinary request information such as IP addresses and browser details under GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Information you send for support

If you email us or open a GitHub issue, we receive the contact details and message you choose to provide and use them to respond, investigate a problem, or improve SyncShow. GitHub issues are public. Do not include OAuth tokens, private Drive links, confidential presentations, or other information you do not want disclosed. Support correspondence is separate from the application’s automatic Google Drive connection.

6. Disclosure and sharing

We do not sell, rent, license, or disclose Google user data to advertisers, data brokers, or unrelated third parties. SyncShow does not share presentation content with its developer.

Data may be handled in these limited ways:

  • Google: to authorize the connection and return the Drive data you requested, under Google’s terms and privacy practices;
  • Local presentation software: Microsoft PowerPoint or LibreOffice may process a selected file locally when used as SyncShow’s converter;
  • People and screens you choose: the operator can deliberately project or stream presentation content using configured outputs; and
  • Legal necessity: we may respond to a valid legal process, but because Google data remains on your computer and Google Drive, we ordinarily do not possess it.

SyncShow’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Retention and deletion

  • OAuth credentials: remain encrypted on the computer until you disconnect the Drive source, clear the application’s local data, or the token is revoked or expires.
  • Access tokens: remain in memory only for the running session and are refreshed as needed.
  • Offline service copies: remain locally after disconnecting so an already-prepared service is not unexpectedly destroyed. They stay until you replace them or delete SyncShow’s local application data.
  • Public-folder content: may also remain in the local offline service copy until replaced or deleted.
  • Support messages: may be retained as reasonably needed to answer the request, document a resolution, protect the project, or meet legal obligations. You may ask us to delete non-public correspondence, subject to applicable requirements.

To stop private Drive access, disconnect the source in Admin Settings. SyncShow removes its local credential record and attempts to revoke the Google token. You can also revoke access at any time from your Google Account’s third-party connections page.

To remove every local copy, quit SyncShow and delete its application-data directory, or contact us for platform-specific help. Uninstalling an application does not always remove its user-data directory. Because we do not operate a SyncShow content server, we cannot remotely inspect or delete files stored on your computer.

8. Security

SyncShow uses the system browser for Google authorization, OAuth PKCE and state protection, loopback-only callbacks, encrypted local refresh-token storage, bounded downloads, integrity checks, and a least-privilege Drive scope. No security measure is perfect. Protect the computer account, operating-system keychain, Google account, and public links you use with SyncShow.

9. Your choices and responsibilities

  • Use local files instead of Google Drive.
  • Use a public view-only folder link without signing in.
  • Select a different private folder or disconnect at any time.
  • Keep Drive publishing disabled; it is off by default.
  • Control which outputs, streams, or people receive presentation content.

If a church or another organization provides the computer or Google account, that organization may have its own privacy obligations and policies. Operators should have permission to use and display the files they load.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when SyncShow’s features or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top will be revised. If a change materially expands how Google user data is used, SyncShow will require appropriate notice and consent before using that data for the new purpose.
