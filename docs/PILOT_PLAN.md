# SyncShow + Heritage Community one-service pilot

## Product contract

SyncShow and Heritage Community edit one canonical, revisioned service
document. Community is the shared authority. SyncShow keeps an offline working
copy and outbox, compiles one exact revision into an immutable ShowPackage, and
adds venue/display controls. Heritage Study Bible consumes manager-approved
sermon publications and songs whose Community access was chosen by the hosting
church.

The pilot must prove this weekly path:

`prepare once -> run reliably -> publish selectively -> rediscover later`

## Delivery checkpoints

1. Preserve the two existing dirty worktrees and curate useful work forward
   without resetting or cleaning either checkout.
2. Introduce `HeritageServiceDocumentV1` and use it as the only editable service
   record in Community and SyncShow.
3. Provide the same order, content, logical-output, and preset editing model in
   the Community web editor and SyncShow. Physical display routing stays local.
4. Rebuild the complete July 26 service natively for English, Russian, and
   Media, retaining the original PowerPoints only as reviewed evidence and an
   emergency fallback.
5. Compile an exact document revision into an offline ShowPackage and preserve
   acknowledged, fail-closed live navigation and simple volunteer controls.
6. Produce internal Windows, macOS, and Linux packages that pass one shared
   workflow smoke test; physical multi-display proof is required on the actual
   venue system only.
7. Finish with manager-reviewed multilingual sermon text/audio publication,
   Heritage primary/mentioned-passage discovery, and one church-selected song
   share.
8. Rehearse against staging and stop at the supervised venue/human-approval
   gate before the first live service.

## Scope boundaries

- No second Community-plan versus SyncShow-project workflow.
- No AI dependency, volunteer scheduling, service telemetry, cloud relay,
  YouTube timestamp automation, public installer signing, or website-specific
  publishing adapter in this pilot.
- Service documents are archived privately. Finish Show never auto-publishes.
- Pastor originals remain private; publication uses an editable reviewed copy
  and may expose an explicitly approved original download.
- Missing songs can be drafted in SyncShow and explicitly submitted to
  Community with credits, relationships, and an access policy. License numbers,
  rights evidence, and rights approval are optional church records and never a
  software gate; the hosting church remains responsible for compliance.

## Validation discipline

- Each checkpoint ends in a visible workflow and a small focused test set.
- Broad suites run after the shared-document migration, after the complete
  historical rehearsal, and before packaging/deployment.
- Preserve critical Show, storage, migration, authorization, and publication
  safety tests. Do not build a generalized proof harness when the real UI or a
  smaller contract check proves the checkpoint.
- If two consecutive cycles only repair tests without advancing a visible
  acceptance criterion, decide whether the proof is release-critical or defer
  it.

## Human gates

The software can prepare a pilot candidate, staging deployment, packages, and
venue checklist. A person must approve the pastor intake, native service
fidelity, venue rehearsal, live service operation, recording, and final public
publication.
