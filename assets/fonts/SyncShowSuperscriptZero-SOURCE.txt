SyncShow Superscript Zero 1.0 (SIL Open Font License 1.1, see OFL-LiberationSans.txt)
A one-glyph font (U+2070 SUPERSCRIPT ZERO) derived from the bundled Liberation Sans 2.1.5 Regular and Bold.
Liberation Sans has superscript one to nine but no superscript zero. The glyph is Liberation's "0" scaled and
raised with the transform that maps Liberation's "1" to its superscript one (U+00B9), with the same advance.
Generated by scripts/fonts/build-superscript-zero.py. It does not use the Reserved Font Name "Liberation".
